How to play

Five letters. Six tries.

Guess a five-letter word, then press Enter. Use your keyboard or the letters on screen.

  • Green: right letter, right place.
  • Gold: right letter, different place.
  • Dark: no remaining match for this letter.

Each round uses a random word from our own answer pool, including every answer in the original Wordle list. The guess dictionary also includes the original Wordle accepted guesses. This is not the New York Times daily puzzle.

Progress resumes when you return to this browser. Share your colored grid without revealing the word, or save an image of the board with letters.
